The card no longer shows a 5-hour bar for an account that has no 5-hour limit.

## Changed

- **No 5-hour bar without a 5-hour limit.** When an account's responses carry a 7-day window and no 5-hour window (the `noFiveHour` evidence 0.10.11 records, such as an Ultra account), the card leaves out its 5-hour segment and the compact line drops its `5h` part; the weekly segment is unchanged. `chottag statusline --json` gains `noFiveHour` (absent when false), and `chottag status` shows `none` in the 5h column for that account. A 5-hour figure that is only unknown, with no reading yet or from an older daemon, shows as before. See [`chottag statusline`](../commands.md#chottag-statusline).
