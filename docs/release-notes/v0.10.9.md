The mod's card now shows whether each usage window runs ahead of the clock, and which account would serve next.

## Added

- **The next account.** The card reads `Serving C → next B`: the account auto-switch would move to on a limit, or the one `chottag next` would pick while auto-switch is off. `→ next: none ready` (yellow) says no account could take over. Under `spread` there is no one next account, so it is left out. `chottag statusline --json` carries it as `next`.

## Changed

- **The usage bars show the pace.** A bar's percent alone did not say whether the window would last until its reset: 85% of a weekly window five days from its reset is a wall, 85% of one an hour from it is fine. Each bar now lays two bars over each other, the window's use and how much of the window has gone by. Cells both cover are `█`, in the usage colour as before (green, yellow from 70%, red from 90%). Cells used ahead of the clock are `▓` in magenta: the limit comes before the reset, sooner the more there are. Time gone but not used is `▒` in dim cyan: slack. The bar is 10 cells and now follows the percent: `5-hour 81% ███████▓░░ resets in 1h 35m (5:30 PM)`. See [the card](../commands.md#the-card).
