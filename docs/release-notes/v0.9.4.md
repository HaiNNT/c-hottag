`chottag status` now shows when each account's 5h and 7d windows reset.

## Changed

- `chottag status` ends each 5h and 7d cell with `↻` and the time of the window's next reset, in your local time: `28% ↻ 3h20m`, or `99% (16h ago) ↻ Fri 18:00` for an older reading. A cell with no known reset, a reset already past, or `unknown` shows none. `--json` is unchanged.

## Fixed

- After a logout or a WindowServer crash, a daemon left in the dead login session no longer marks every account `needs-login`. A not-found or denied keychain read first checks that the keychains are reachable, and two accounts that worked and stop reading together are treated the same way: the accounts read `stale`, with no mark and no notice. A minute or two later the daemon exits (unless a supervisor runs it) and the next `claude` starts a fresh one. The first `needs-login` read of a slot logs its `exit status N`.
- A `passthrough: token needs-login` (or `token stale`) mark no longer outlives a fresh login. A re-login, a token renewal or a usage poll that works clears it, a daemon start drops the previous daemon's marks, and `chottag status` does not show a `token needs-login` mark older than the account's last login. This mostly affected a rotation-off account, which only an own-token response used to clear.
