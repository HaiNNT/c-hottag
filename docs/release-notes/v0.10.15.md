A refused account login is now marked, shown and taken out of rotation.

## Fixed

- **No more misleading "a new route was refused" notice for an account-wide refusal.** When Anthropic refused an account's login, a request on a route chottag's table does not list was reported as a new route being refused, and counted as route drift. chottag now holds such a refusal until it knows whether the account works (a 2xx on a listed serving route, such as `/v1/messages`, settles it as a route refusal; a login refusal on a serving request settles it as the account's). The route notice can therefore come up to 10 minutes later, and a refused account's refusals are not counted.

## Changed

- **A refused login marks the account.** After one 401 or 403 on serving requests (following the refresh and retry), the account is marked `login refused`: `chottag status` shows `login refused (403) since 15:04`, `status --json` has `loginRefused` and `loginRefusedStatus` on the account, and `chottag doctor`'s `token:NAME` row is a problem (`chottag login NAME`). The daemon log has a line when the mark is set and when it clears.
- **A marked account is out of rotation.** Auto-switch, `chottag next`, `chottag move`, spread placement and the status line's `next` skip it, with reason `login refused`. With auto on, a serving account that is marked is switched off (trigger `refused`, with a notice), and spread sessions leave it. `chottag next --force` and `chottag tag` still work.
- **How the mark clears.** A 2xx on the account's own login, a successful usage poll (about every 30 minutes for an idle account; longer after the poll's own refusals back off), or `chottag login NAME`, the immediate fix. A token renewal does not. One transient refusal can bench an account until then.
